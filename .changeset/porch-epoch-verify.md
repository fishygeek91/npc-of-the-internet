The porch verifies each Wanderer line against that epoch's arrival session key. The open epoch stays live speech, and a past epoch is marked as an earlier visit. A line newer than the verified snapshot stays checking until a debounced refetch, plus two short retries, has completed after the line was shown. Reaction chips follow that same checking, earlier-visit, and failed treatment.
